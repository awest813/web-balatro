Balatro

Play Balatro in your browser, using your own copy of the game.

  1. Choose Balatro.exe from your install folder (where is it?).
  2. Click Build. This takes a few seconds and only has to be done once.
  3. Click Start Game.

Everything runs locally: your game files and saves stay in this browser and are never uploaded.
